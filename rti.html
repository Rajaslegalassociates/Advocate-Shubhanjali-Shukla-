Right to Information

RTI Applications, Appeals & Related Matters

The Right to Information Act establishes a statutory framework for seeking information from public authorities, subject to its provisions, exemptions and applicable procedures.

The appropriate procedure depends on the public authority concerned, the nature of the information sought, statutory timelines and the circumstances of the matter.

  • Preparation and review of RTI applications
  • First appeals under the applicable statutory framework
  • Second appeals before the appropriate authority
  • Information-related representations
  • Research concerning relevant statutory provisions
  • Related proceedings, where applicable
