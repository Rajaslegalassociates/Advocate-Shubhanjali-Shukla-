FAQ

Common Questions

What is an RTI application and how is it filed?

Under the Right to Information Act, a person may submit a written application, with the prescribed fee, to the Public Information Officer of a public authority seeking specific information. If no reply is received or it is unsatisfactory, a first appeal and then a second appeal may be available, within the timelines under the Act.

What is bail?

Bail is the conditional release of an accused person while a case is pending. Whether it can be granted depends on the facts of the case, the applicable law and the court's discretion.

Which documents should I bring for a consultation?

Any identity proof, and papers related to the matter, such as notices, orders, agreements, property papers or an FIR copy, along with a short written timeline of events.
